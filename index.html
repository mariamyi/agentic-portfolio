About

Care-centered digital humanities, against erasure

I build digital scholarship and preservation infrastructure for community-engaged research — platforms, archives, and interactive experiences that make complex histories accessible, searchable, and durable. My work treats the digital humanities as a site of resistance to digital erasure, foregrounding care, community collaboration, and ethical, long-term stewardship of records and stories.

As Digital Projects Coordinator at Virginia Tech University Libraries, I serve as technical lead and co-investigator across projects spanning oral history, material culture, mapping, and multisensory storytelling. I came to this work through multimedia journalism (B.A., The American University in Cairo) and communications & advocacy (M.A., James Madison University), and I bring a critical lens on Middle East politics, contemporary movements, and revolutions to the scholarship I support.
